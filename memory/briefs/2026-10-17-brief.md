## AI chips & compute infrastructure
- **China proposed adding 23 AI technologies to its export-control list** (draft, Oct 16), covering large models, agents, chip designs and computing clusters. TNW reports the draft would apply to models with ≥2T parameters or top-10 rankings, and to design know-how for chips at ≤14nm, with open-source code exempt and comments due Nov 15. *So what:* Beijing is copying the US playbook. Non-Chinese buyers of Chinese models or chip IP should expect licensing friction. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-16/china-proposes-export-controls-on-ai-models-chips-and-agents) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/china-ai-export-controls-draft)
- **Taiwan will add AI servers and advanced semiconductors to its controlled-items list** (Oct 16). Shipping restricted AI hardware to China will become a criminal offense under the Foreign Trade Act, as part of a US trade deal. *So what:* this closes the gap that let smugglers be charged only with offences like forgery. Expect tighter compliance at Taiwanese server makers (ODMs). [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-16/taiwan-to-curb-ai-chip-exports-to-china-as-part-of-us-trade-deal) · [Taipei Times, June background](https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/biz/archives/2026/06/10/2003858815)

Signals to watch: the final text of both measures (China's comment period closes Nov 15), and any US response on chips rented offshore.

## Quantum computing commercialisation
- **Oratomic (Pasadena, neutral atoms) raised a $475M Series B at a reported $5.4B valuation**, up from $1.5B about three months earlier. ARCH, Spark, Khosla, Index, General Catalyst and Bezos Expeditions led the round. *So what:* its pitch is that useful machines need ~10k qubits rather than ~1M. If that holds, the timeline for the whole field gets shorter. One source gives the valuation as $5.5B. [Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/news/161158-oratomic-raises-475m-series-b-hitting-5-4b-three-months-after-its-series/) · [WSJ via Placera](https://www.placera.se/telegram/kvantdatorstartupen-oratomic-varderas-till-5-4-miljarder-dollar-efter-ny-runda-wsj-20261008)
- **Universal Quantum (UK, trapped ions) raised a $100M+ Series A** co-led by DCVC and Firgun Ventures, with Singapore's EDBI participating (Oct 8). The money funds an R&D centre in Singapore and expansion in the US, Japan and Hamburg. *So what:* this is the largest UK quantum Series A, and sovereign funds are now co-investing alongside VCs. [University of Sussex](https://www.sussex.ac.uk/research/centres/sussex-centre-for-quantum-technologies/news-and-events/news?id=71456) · [Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2026/10/08/university-of-sussex-quantum-spinout-raises-record-ps100m-series-a/) · [The Star/Bloomberg](https://www.thestar.com.my/business/business-news/2026/10/09/roblox-ceo-spore-govt-fund-back-universal-quantum-round)
- **Jülich and RIKEN expanded their HPC and quantum partnership** (Oct 15). [HPCwire](https://www.hpcwire.com/off-the-wire/julich-and-riken-expand-partnership-in-hpc-and-quantum-computing/)

Signals to watch: Gartner expects more than half of quantum startups to close by 2030, while valuations are rising fast. Watch for consolidation.

## European deep-tech funding
- **Salience Labs (Oxford) raised a $125M Series B led by Bullhound Capital**, with AMD Ventures, Intel Capital and the British Business Bank participating (Oct 14). The company makes optical circuit switches that connect thousands of GPUs across racks. *So what:* chipmakers are investing directly in optical interconnect, a sign that networking is becoming the bottleneck in AI clusters. [Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2026/10/14/salience-labs-secures-125m-to-scale-photonic-chips-for-ai-data-centres/) · [Optics.org](https://optics.org/news/uks-salience-labs-adds-125m-series-b) · [SDxCentral](https://www.sdxcentral.com/news/amd-intel-back-optical-upstart-pushing-for-denser-photonics-inside-ai-racks/)
- **HyLight (France) raised €13M to industrialise autonomous hydrogen airships**, with plans to grow its fleet from 4 to 15 (Oct 13). [Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2026/10/13/french-deeptech-hylight-raises-eur13m-to-industrialise-autonomous-hydrogen-powered-airships/)
